Refund Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-07 | Last updated: 2026-10-07

1. Business Campaign Refunds

Unspent Budget

Campaigns operate on an escrow model. Your budget is fully funded before a campaign goes live. Unspent funds are automatically returned to you when:

  • The campaign reaches its end date
  • The campaign is paused by you
  • The campaign auto-pauses at 90% budget utilization (hard ceiling)
  • The campaign is settled after all tasks are verified

Refunds of unspent escrow are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method.

Disputed Outcomes

  • If a task outcome is disputed and the dispute is upheld in your favor, the outcome amount is credited back to your campaign budget.
  • Dispute resolution follows a 72-hour SLA with a named reviewer.
  • Credited amounts remain in your campaign escrow for future tasks or can be refunded on request.

Campaign Cancellation

  • You may cancel a campaign before it goes live for a full refund.
  • After a campaign goes live, you may pause it. Unspent funds are refundable.
  • Already-verified and paid outcomes are not refundable.

2. Promoter Reward Disputes

  • Promoters may raise disputes on task verification outcomes within 7 days of the verification decision.
  • Disputes follow the lifecycle: open → under review → upheld/rejected/withdrawn/expired.
  • If upheld, the reward is paid per the original task terms.
  • If rejected, the original verification stands. No refund of "lost" rewards — rewards are only paid for checked results.
  • Fraudulent submissions result in clawback of any paid rewards and account suspension.

3. Data Bundle Fulfillment Issues

  • Data bundles (MTN/Glo/Airtel/9mobile) are fulfilled via a licensed aggregator.
  • If a bundle fails to deliver due to aggregator error, we will retry once automatically.
  • If retry fails, the reward is re-credited to your wallet for re-fulfillment or cash conversion (if KYC Tier 2 + age verified).
  • Network detection errors: if we detect the wrong network, contact support within 48 hours for correction.

4. Cash Payout Issues

  • Cash payouts require KYC Tier 2 + age verification, minimum ₦1,000 balance.
  • Payouts are batched per schedule (settings.payout.batch_size).
  • If a payout fails due to provider error (bank/OPay/Kuda/PalmPay), we retry per the idempotency key.
  • Failed payouts after retries are held in your wallet. Contact support for manual resolution.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • Verified and paid task outcomes (clicks, sign-ups, promo-code uses).
  • Data bundles successfully delivered to the verified number.
  • Cash payouts successfully settled to the verified account.
  • Fraud reserve (5% of campaign budget) — released after campaign settlement if no fraud.

6. Refund Request Process

  1. Email refunds@promota.ng with: campaign ID / task ID, reason, amount.
  2. We acknowledge within 24 hours and investigate within 5 business days.
  3. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 business days.
  4. Disputed decisions may be escalated per the dispute resolution process.

7. Regulatory Compliance

This policy complies with the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) 2018 and NDPA 2023. Nigerian consumer rights are respected in full.
