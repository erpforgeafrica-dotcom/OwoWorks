How it works · 3 steps

Your path to data and cash

  1. Join with your phone

    Enter your name and Nigerian phone number. Get your personal invite code instantly. No follower count needed. No social links required.

  2. Complete tasks, earn points

    Browse available tasks. Each shows exactly how many points you earn. Post, share, click, or verify — every completed task adds points to your balance.

  3. Redeem points for data or cash

    Points convert to MTN, Glo, or Airtel bundles instantly on your own number. Cash unlocks as you grow through levels. Referral points boost your progress — especially after level 3.

Tasks & Points

Simple tasks, clear points

Social tasks

Share a post, comment, follow a brand account. Points awarded per completed action. No fake engagement — only verifiable actions count.

Verification tasks

Confirm a business location, test a USSD code, screenshot a promo. Higher points for tasks needing physical presence or app interaction.

Referral boost

Invite friends with your code. You earn referral points when they join and complete their first tasks. After level 3, referrals are required to unlock the next level.
