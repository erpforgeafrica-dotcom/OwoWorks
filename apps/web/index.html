Virtual jobs · simple tasks

How a promoter earns

  1. Verify your phone

    One-time code by SMS or WhatsApp. We detect your network so data goes to the right line.

  2. Link your account

    Connect YouTube or Facebook so we can confirm the post is really yours. Minimum 200 followers at pilot.

  3. Take a task

    We give you a caption, a tracked link and a disclosure tag. You post it as yourself. Every task shows its exact naira price before you start.

  4. Get checked, then paid

    Automated checks, plus a human review of roughly one in ten. 48 to 72 hour hold, then data or cash. If the poster stays silent for 24 hours, your submission is approved automatically — your money never hangs on someone else's delay.

Pricing

Final prices are not published yet

Publishing numbers before the pilot would be a guess, and we will not invent them. Pilot partners get written pricing once the margin model is measured.

How promoter pay works

Each checked outcome has a price. The promoter share, a fraud reserve, and the platform share come out of it. The split is fixed in code and tested, not decided per payout.

What sets the price

Supply and demand per platform and niche, inside a floor and ceiling that keep margin above the configured minimum.

Withdrawal rules

Data rewards go only to your own verified numbers. Cash needs identity checks, a minimum balance and a batched payout schedule.
