1. Overview

Promota ("we", "us", "our") operates a promotion platform connecting businesses with verified Nigerian promoters. These Terms govern your access to and use of our website and services.

By accessing or using Promota, you agree to be bound by these Terms. If you disagree with any part, you may not use our services.

2. Definitions

Promoter
A verified Nigerian individual who promotes campaigns from their own social media accounts.
Business
An entity that funds campaigns on Promota.
Campaign
A promotion task with defined outcomes (clicks, sign-ups, promo-code uses) and a price per outcome.
Checked Result
An outcome verified by automated checks and/or human review.

3. Promoter Eligibility

  • Must be a natural person, 18 years or older.
  • Must be resident in Nigeria.
  • Must verify phone number via SMS/WhatsApp OTP.
  • Must connect a social media account (YouTube/Facebook) with minimum 200 followers.
  • One identity per person. Duplicate accounts are prohibited.

4. How Promoters Earn

  1. Complete assigned tasks: post caption + tracked link + #ad disclosure.
  2. Outcomes are verified (automated + 10% human review sample).
  3. 48–72 hour hold after verification, then rewards are paid.
  4. Auto-approval after 24 hours if the campaign owner takes no action.
  5. Data rewards (MTN/Glo/Airtel bundles) go only to your own verified number.
  6. Cash rewards require KYC Tier 2 + age verification, minimum ₦1,000 balance, batched payouts.

5. Anti-MLM / Anti-Pyramid Commitment

  • No joining fee. No payment for recruiting. No income promises. Ever.
  • Referrals move you up the queue only. Referrals pay ZERO cash.
  • Ambassador contracts are capped, written, tied to margin + paid work only.

6. Business Terms

  • Budget must be fully funded before a campaign goes live (escrow model).
  • Campaigns auto-pause at 90% of budget. Hard ceiling, not a promise.
  • Unspent funds return to the business.
  • Pay per checked click, sign-up, or promo-code use. Follows, likes, views are never billed.
  • Every post carries #ad disclosure.

7. Disputes

  • Promoters may raise disputes on task outcomes within 7 days.
  • Disputes follow a 72-hour SLA: open → under review → upheld/rejected/withdrawn/expired.
  • Resolution requires a named reviewer. Decisions are final.
